What we see in Nashville garages

Common reasons Middle Tennessee garage doors fail.

Springs wear out on a schedule

Standard torsion springs are commonly rated for around 10,000 open-and-close cycles. At four trips a day, that's under seven years. If your door is that old and still on its original springs, a break is a matter of when.

Spring storm season

Middle Tennessee gets strong storms and high winds in spring. If debris, a branch or a backing car hit your door, don't force it open or closed. Bent tracks and loose cables can drop the door.

Humidity and rust

Humid summers are hard on rollers, hinges and steel hardware. A door that groans or shakes usually needs lubrication or worn rollers replaced, and it's a cheap fix compared to a failed part.

Older openers in older neighborhoods

Homes in places like East Nashville, Inglewood, Donelson and Madison often still have very old openers. Some lack photo-eye safety sensors entirely, and we'd recommend upgrading those.

What does a spring replacement cost?
It depends on your door's weight, the spring size, and whether one spring or a pair is replaced. Call with your door size and we'll quote it over the phone.
Can I replace a broken spring myself?
We strongly advise against it. Springs and cables are under extreme tension and can cause serious injury. Leave the door closed, keep people clear, and call a professional.
A storm damaged my door. What should I do?
Keep people and pets away from it, and don't try to force the door open or closed. Take photos, then call us. We'll tell you whether it's a track, cable or panel problem and what it costs to fix.
What's included in a tune-up?
We lubricate moving parts, test balance, test the safety reverse and photo eyes, tighten hardware, and inspect springs, cables and rollers.
